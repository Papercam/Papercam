# Papercam for Windows

Papercam turns your webcam into a live pencil sketch, so you can join Zoom, Teams, Google Meet or Discord without showing your real face or your room. The sketch is drawn on your own computer: your camera picture is never uploaded.

**Download:** https://papercam.app/download
**Website:** https://papercam.app

This repository only hosts the Windows installers. The app keeps itself up to date from here automatically.

## Installing

1. Download `Papercam-Setup.exe` from the latest release (or from the website).
2. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. In your meeting app, open the camera setting and choose **Papercam**.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
